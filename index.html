Anime Auction Draft

Anime Auction Draft (Enchères Animés in French) is a free game in which two players build teams of anime characters by auction. Each round one player draws a character card and sees it, the other does not, and both bid from the same fictional budget. The player who drew the card can bluff. When the cards run out, the stronger team wins.

Play on one device against the computer or a friend, in a private online room, or in ranked quick matches with trophies, leagues, leaderboards and friends. Signing in with Google is optional and only keeps your profile across devices.
