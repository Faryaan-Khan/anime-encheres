Anime Auction Draft

Privacy policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Anime Auction Draft (Enchères Animés in French) is a free, unofficial fan game at anime-encheres.pages.dev. This policy explains what the game stores about you, why, and what you can do about it. It is short because the game collects very little.

What the game stores

  • A device identifier. Your browser creates a random code the first time you play. The game's server keeps only a one-way hash of it, which it uses to recognise your device and your profile.
  • Your profile. The nickname, avatar and country you choose (the country is optional), your trophies, statistics, badges, season results and your player tag.
  • Your games. Online games are recorded with every move, both teams and the result, to judge rankings fairly and to detect cheating.
  • Friends. Who you have added as a friend and invitations to private rooms.
  • Feedback. Messages you send through the feedback form, and a contact only if you type one.
  • Abuse protection. A one-way hash of your network address, kept for up to two days to limit how often one connection can create rooms, send feedback or try recovery codes. The address itself is never stored.
  • On your device. The game keeps your device code, language, theme, sound setting and an unfinished offline game in your browser's local storage. It sets no advertising or tracking cookies.

Signing in with Google

Playing as a guest needs no account. If you choose to sign in with Google, the game asks Google only for your basic profile and email address (the openid, email and profile scopes). Our authentication provider, Supabase, keeps your Google account identifier, email address and the name and picture Google returns, so that the same Google account always opens the same game profile. The game uses this information only for that purpose. It does not show your email address or Google name to other players, does not send you email, and does not access any other Google data such as Gmail, Drive or contacts.

The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Google user data is never sold, never used for advertising and never shared with anyone except the service providers below, which store it on our behalf.

Who processes the data

  • Supabase hosts the game's database and authentication.
  • Netlify serves the website and, like any web host, processes requests to deliver pages.

The game shows no advertising and has no analytics or third-party trackers.

What other players see

Your nickname, avatar, country (if you set one), player tag, trophies and ranks can appear on leaderboards, in friends lists and to your opponents. Nothing else about you is shown.

How long data is kept

Profiles, games and season results are kept while the game runs, so that rankings and records stay accurate. Hashed network addresses are deleted after two days. Unused online rooms are deleted after a day.

Your choices

  • You can change your nickname, avatar and country at any time on your profile.
  • You can sign out on a device; that device then starts a new guest profile.
  • To have your profile and its games deleted, or to ask what is stored about you, send a request through the feedback form with your player tag. It is handled within 30 days.
  • You can remove the game's access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Children

The game is meant for players aged 13 and over. If you are under the age of majority where you live, play with a parent's or guardian's permission. If you believe a child under 13 has created a profile, ask us to delete it through the feedback form.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes too. Significant changes are announced in the game.

Contact

Questions about privacy: use the feedback form.
